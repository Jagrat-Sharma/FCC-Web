BEFORE THE FIRST PLANK GOES DOWN

A little planning.
A clearer project.

Whether you’re replacing one room or updating a business, these details help us have a useful first conversation.

Find our showroom and contact details

  1. Tell us about the space

    Share your location, approximate room sizes, photos and how the space is used. Let us know about stairs or areas that need special attention.

  2. Talk through the practical details

    Discuss existing flooring, subfloor condition, furniture, access and transitions between rooms. Confirm what preparation and removal are included in your project.

  3. Agree on the scope and timing

    Review your material choice, installation method and schedule with us. Ask about product-specific preparation and when the space can be used again.
