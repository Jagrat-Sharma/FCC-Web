ABOUT FIRST CHOICE CARPETS

A better floor.
A place that
feels like you.

From the carpet on your stairs to the hardwood in your living room, the right floor changes how a space feels. We help you choose it, plan it and bring it home.

Explore our flooring

ROOTED IN PERSONAL SERVICE

There’s more to a floor
than its colour.

It’s where your children play, where guests gather and where your working day begins. Choosing one means thinking about comfort, upkeep, everyday wear and the way you want your space to look.

Family-owned and operated since 1991, First Choice serves homeowners and businesses across Toronto and the GTA from our Brampton showroom. Our range includes carpets, rugs, hardwood, laminate and vinyl, alongside installation services.

Bring us your ideas and questions. We’ll talk through the materials, the installation and the details that matter to your project.

MAKE YOUR VISIT COUNT

Bring a little inspiration.
Leave with a clearer plan.

You don’t need to know the product name or have every measurement ready. A few details help us start the right conversation.

  1. Show us your space

    Bring room photos, approximate dimensions and any colours or finishes you want to keep.

  2. Tell us how you use it

    Busy entryway, quiet bedroom or commercial space? Share your priorities, budget and timing.

  3. Compare it in person

    Look at colours and textures together, feel the materials, and ask about care and installation before deciding.
