# Managing First Choice Carpets

Your website manager is at `https://YOUR-WEBSITE-DOMAIN/admin/`. Your developer will give you the final link and approve your email address. You do not need WordPress, code, or access to Cloudflare's dashboard for everyday changes.

## Sign in

Open the admin link and sign in through Cloudflare Access with your approved email account. Depending on setup, you will use your normal company login or receive a one-time email code. Do not share that code. Your signed-in email appears at the top. Use **Sign out** when finished.

## Add a product

1. Choose **Products → Add product**.
2. Enter its name, select a category, and add a plain-text description.
3. Product entry does not require pricing. New products are enquiry-only.
4. Choose **Featured product** if it should appear first in the default catalogue order.
5. Upload a JPEG, PNG or WebP image. Wait for the upload confirmation. Add a short image description, such as “Natural oak flooring with a matte finish.”
6. Check **Display on website** when ready, or leave it unchecked to save a private draft.
7. Choose **Save changes**. Open the website to verify the product. No rebuild or deployment is needed for content changes.

The image is optimized automatically; source files must be under 20 MB. A product can be saved without an image, but a gallery item needs one. The website displays “Image coming soon” for products without an image.

## Change or remove a product

Find the product using search and choose **Edit**. You can change text, category, featured status, image, and publication status. Save when finished. Turning off Display on website hides it from the public website while retaining it in your dashboard.

Choose **Delete** and confirm only when you want to remove the record permanently. To keep it for later, unpublish it instead. Deleting the record does not automatically remove its image file.

## Update the gallery

Choose **Gallery → Add gallery item**. Add a title, description, image and image description. **Display order** controls position: lower numbers appear first. Items with the same order show newer items first. Publish and save. Use Edit to replace an image or change the caption, or unpublish an item to hide it.

Only upload photos you have permission to publish. The caption is where you can accurately describe a finished installation or an inspiration image.

## Image cleanup

The **Images** tab shows each upload and how many records reference it. Images in use cannot be deleted. After replacing an image or cancelling a new item, the unused upload may remain here; delete it when you are sure it is no longer needed. Draft items also count as references.

## If something goes wrong

- Read the message near the Save button; fix the highlighted/required field and try again.
- If your session expires, copy any unsaved text before using **Sign in again / reload dashboard**.
- If a save reports that the item changed, another tab or editor saved first. Copy your unsaved changes, cancel, refresh the list, reopen the item, and apply your changes to its latest version.
- If your connection drops during a save, refresh the list before adding it again; the first save may already have succeeded.
- If an image upload fails, try a smaller JPEG, PNG or WebP. An upload must finish before you save.
- Use **Refresh** to reload a list after an error. Contact your developer if the problem continues.

Categories, navigation menus, the home/about/contact/FAQ page text, and staff access are managed by your developer. All approved CMS editors can manage products, gallery items and unused images.
